A Seamless Kashmir Journey,
Managed In-House

Your pre-wedding experience in Kashmir doesn’t end with the shoot.

Through The Travado, our own in-house travel company, we can take care of your complete journey — from hand-picked hotels and airport transfers to private transportation and curated local experiences.

With no third-party travel partners and no fragmented communication, everything is coordinated directly by our team.

Our highly experienced local drivers know Kashmir’s routes, timings, key destinations and, importantly, the pre-wedding locations we work with — ensuring smooth and well-timed movement between your hotel, shoot locations and activities.

Everything, arranged
by one team.

Hotels

Hand-picked properties across categories, with room options and images so you can choose with confidence.

Airport Transfers

Met on arrival and moved between your hotel and shoot locations without a handover to anyone else.

Private Transportation

Vehicle categories to suit your group, driven by locals who know the routes and the timings that matter.

Curated Experiences

Activities chosen around your shoot days, so the valley is something you experience rather than pass through.

Once you reach out to us regarding hotels, transfers, transportation or activities, we’ll share everything you need to make an informed choice:

  • Hotel categories
  • Recommended properties
  • Room options
  • Hotel images
  • Vehicle categories
  • Activity options
  • A personalised quotation based on your requirements

From the moment you arrive at the airport to your pre-wedding shoot and everything you choose to explore beyond it, The Travado is designed to make your Kashmir journey effortless, well-coordinated and beautifully planned.
