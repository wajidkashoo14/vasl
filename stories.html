Stories,
Seen Differently

A collection of stories shaped by the people, places, and moments that made them their own.

Between Kashmir’s mountains and meadows, beside the still waters of Dal Lake, through the slopes of Gulmarg and Pahalgam, the forests of Sonamarg, and the open valleys of Doodhpathri and Gurez — every story finds its own rhythm.

In the shade of trees, the movement of wind, the fragrance of the valley, and the quiet between two people — we find moments that cannot be directed, only felt.

These are the stories we have witnessed, understood, and turned into frames.
