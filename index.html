VASL
वस्ल  ·  وصل

An Urdu and Persian word meaning union, togetherness, or the coming together of two souls.

We keep the number of projects we take on deliberately limited.

Good work needs time, attention, and intention. Staying small keeps us personally involved in every story, and ensures growth never comes at the expense of the work.

VASL is a label by Kashmir Wedding.

Our journey began in 2015, when Kashmir Wedding started documenting weddings across Kashmir. Before that, our team had already spent years photographing and exploring Kashmir — its landscapes, people, culture, and ever-changing light. That experience shaped the way we see, photograph, and tell stories.

2015
Where it began

As Kashmir Wedding grew, so did our work. What began in Kashmir eventually took us across India, while our work expanded beyond weddings into pre-weddings, destination celebrations, and brand collaborations.

Along the way, Aamir Beigh and Suhail Beigh have had the opportunity to work with leading Indian and international brands, bringing those experiences into the way we approach photography and films.

2022
VASL is born

We created VASL as a distinct label dedicated to pre-weddings, destination weddings, and films. The idea was simple: to create a space that could be recognised independently for this kind of work, while carrying forward the experience, people, and foundation built through Kashmir Wedding.

2026
Today

We have been telling stories for more than 11 years. Our team has grown, our work has taken us further, and our experience continues to evolve. Yet one thing has remained deliberate: we keep the number of projects we take on limited.

For us, photography and films are not simply about creating beautiful photographs and films. They are about understanding the people in front of the camera — their connection, personalities, comfort, and the moments that make their story uniquely theirs.

Because ultimately, your story matters.

Experience

Built from one story
to the next.

Not just years behind a camera, but years of watching, travelling, listening, understanding, adapting, and learning from people.

Experience is not measured by how expensive the work looks.

It comes from client to client, outfit to outfit, location to location, and conversation to conversation — from learning to listen, observe, and understand what someone needs, sometimes before they find the words to say it.

Since 2015, we have photographed and filmed stories across Kashmir, India, and beyond. Even before Kashmir Wedding, our founders and team members were already behind cameras, exploring photography, travelling, experimenting, and learning through real experiences.

Over the years, working with different clients, cultures, personalities, destinations, and brands has shaped the way we approach every story. No two people are the same, and no two stories should feel the same. Experience has taught us to adapt rather than repeat — to understand the people first and let the photography and films follow naturally.

A wider perspective

Our work with leading brands and our travels across different places have expanded how we see. Every project has taught us something about light, locations, styling, timing, communication, direction, and the small details that often make the biggest difference.

Understanding people

We pay attention to how you communicate, what makes you comfortable, what you naturally gravitate towards, and even what you may not say out loud. Being in front of a camera does not come naturally to everyone. Our role is not simply to tell you where to stand or how to pose — it is to create an environment where you can be yourselves, while knowing when to guide, when to wait, and when to simply observe.

Knowing when not to interfere

Some of the most meaningful photographs happen between the planned moments — a glance, a laugh, a pause, a quiet walk, or a spontaneous interaction. Recognising those moments comes from years of paying attention.

A different approach, every time

Every location brings a different language. Every couple brings a different energy. Every story requires a different approach.

That is the experience we bring to every story.
